    Formulalar

    • Rezerford formulasi: dσ/dΩ = (zZe²/(4πε₀·4E))² / sin⁴(θ/2)
    • Sanoq tezligi: n(θ) = I₀ · nat · ΔΩ · dσ/dΩ, demak n·sin⁴(θ/2) = K = const
    • Yadro zaryadi: Z = (4E / ze²k) · √(K / (I₀ nat ΔΩ)), ke² = 1,44 MeV·fm
    • Nishon masofasi: tg(θ/2) = d/(2b), d = zZke²/E
    • Eng yaqin masofa: rmin = (d/2)(1 + 1/sin(θ/2))

    Yadro qo‘zg‘almas va nuqtaviy deb olinadi, α-zarra folgada bir marta sochiladi (folga yupqa). Elektronlarning ekranlashi va yadro kuchlari hisobga olinmaydi. Sanoq — Puasson statistikasiga bo‘ysunadigan tasodifiy son, xatoligi ΔN = √N.
