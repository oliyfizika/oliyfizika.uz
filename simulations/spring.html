Ko‘rinish

Maslahat: yukni sudrab siljiting va qo‘yib yuboring. T(m) grafigini bosib massani tanlash mumkin.

Nima bo‘lyapti?

Yuk prujinaga mahkamlangan: siljiganda prujina uni muvozanat nuqtasi tomon tortadi (Guk qonuni). Gorizontal yoki vertikal tebranishni tanlab, massa, qattiqlik, so‘nish va tashqi kuch ta’sirini kuzating.

Formulalar

  • Guk qonuni: F = −k x
  • Harakat tenglamasi: m x″ = −k x − b x′ + F₀ cos Ωt
  • Tebranish davri: T = 2π √(m/k), ω₀ = √(k/m), f = 1/T
  • Vertikal: Δl = mg/k — muvozanat siljiydi, davr o‘zgarmaydi
  • Energiya: E = mv²/2 + kx²/2 = kA²/2
  • Parallel: k = k₁ + k₂; ketma-ket: k = k₁k₂/(k₁ + k₂)
  • So‘nish: γ = b/m, ωd = √(ω₀² − γ²/4), bkr = 2√(km)
  • Rezonans: A = (F₀/m) / √((ω₀² − Ω²)² + γ²Ω²)

Prujina vaznsiz va Guk qonuniga bo‘ysunadi; silliq sirt (gorizontalda). Integrallash: 4-tartibli Runge–Kutta.
