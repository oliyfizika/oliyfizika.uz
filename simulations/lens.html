Formulalar

  • Yupqa linza formulasi: 1/F = 1/d + 1/f (mavhum tasvirda f < 0, sochuvchi linzada F < 0)
  • Optik kuch: D = 1/F (dioptriya, F metrda)
  • Chiziqli kattalashtirish: Γ = H/h = f/d
  • Lupa: Γ = d₀/F, d₀ = 25 cm — eng yaxshi ko‘rish masofasi
  • Mikroskop: Γ ≈ (Δ/F₁)·(d₀/F₂); teleskop: Γ = F₁/F₂

Paraksial (o‘qqa yaqin nurlar) yaqinlashish, yupqa linzalar. Ko‘z — soddalashtirilgan model: optik kuchi 58,8–62,8 D (akkomodatsiya 4 D), ko‘zoynak ko‘zdan 12 mm oldinda.
