Formulalar

  • Bλ = (2hc²/λ⁵)/(ehc/λkT − 1) — Plank formulasi
  • λmax·T = b = 2,898·10⁻³ m·K — Vin siljish qonuni
  • M = σT⁴, σ = 2π⁵k⁴/(15c²h³) — Stefan–Bolsman qonuni
  • E = εσ(T⁴ − T₀⁴)·a²/L² — radiometr ko‘rsatkichi

b va σ dan Plank doimiysi h va Bolsman doimiysi k ni hisoblash mumkin — kvant nazariyasining boshlanishi aynan qora jism spektridan bo‘lgan.
